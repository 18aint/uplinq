## 🎨 **Additional Design Tips for LTX Studio**

1. **Typography Hierarchy:**
   - Headline: 48-64px bold
   - Subheading: 24-32px medium
   - Supporting text: 18-24px regular

2. **Brand Consistency:**
   - Always use Uplinq's blue palette
   - Maintain clean, professional aesthetic
   - Include subtle gradients for visual interest

3. **Social Media Optimization:**
   - Ensure text is readable on mobile
   - High contrast for accessibility
   - Eye-catching but not overwhelming

4. **Call-to-Action Focus:**
   - Each image should have clear purpose
   - Direct messaging for conversion
